# Landing page design: examples

All examples use one fictional gym chain's membership page, "Northside Gym". Every figure is a placeholder to be replaced with a verified one.

## 1. The two-pass plan

**Pass 1: draft**

- Success: completed free-trial bookings per visit.
- Palette: Forest `#1d4a3b` (action), Chalk `#f4f2ec` (light sections), Iron `#151719` (dark sections), Clay `#c8643b` (small accents), Fog `#8a8f96` (muted).
- Type: a condensed sans for headlines, a plain sans for body.
- Memorable element: the hero is a real timetable that animates today's classes filling up.
- Sections:

```
[ Hero: "Train with people who know your name" · timetable · Book a free trial ]
[ Proof: 4 clubs · 120 classes a week · 4.8 on Google (link) ]
[ Problem: busy gyms, no one notices you ]
[ Solution: small classes, the same trainers, a plan ]
[ Who it's for ]  [ Trainers ]  [ What's included ]
[ Pricing: 3 plans, full terms ]  [ FAQ ]  [ Final CTA ]
```

**Pass 2: check against the brief**

- "A hero photo of a smiling athlete" would fit any gym, so it's replaced by the live timetable, which is specific to Northside.
- A dark band with a numbered eyebrow on every section is a template habit. The numbers are dropped, and dark is kept only for the Trainers section.

## 2. One page, three variations

The content and section order are fixed. Only the look changes.

| | Chalk & forest | Timetable | Big type |
|---|---|---|---|
| Direction | Editorial | Product | Bold graphic |
| Hero | Split: real club photo and headline | The animated live timetable | Type only: "SMALL CLASSES." |
| Rhythm | Bands alternating chalk and iron | Continuous white | Colour blocks of forest and clay |
| Type | A serif display with a sans | One sans | A heavy condensed display with a sans |
| Imagery | Real club photography | App and timetable screenshots | Cut-out trainer portraits |
| Motion | One slow hero reveal | The timetable filling up | Snappy section transitions |
| Pick when | The brand sells atmosphere | Visitors compare schedules and price | A launch or a younger audience |

## 3. Hero copy

```
Train with people who know your name.
Small classes, the same trainers every week, and a plan that fits your diary.

[ Book a free trial ]   See prices ↓

Monthly £39 · cancel anytime · 4 clubs in Leeds
```

The price and the cancellation terms are visible from the hero, and "See prices" jumps to the full pricing.

## 4. Honest and dishonest microcopy

| Dishonest | Honest |
|---|---|
| "Only 3 spots left!" (always shown) | "Spin 45 on Tue has 3 spots left" (live data), or nothing |
| "Join 10,000+ happy members" (unverified) | "1,240 members across 4 clubs, Oct 2026" |
| "From £1/day" | "£39/month, billed monthly. 12-month plan £390. Cancel with 30 days' notice." |
| "No thanks, I don't want to get fit" | "No thanks" |
| A star rating with no source | "4.8 on Google from 312 reviews" (linked) |

## 5. A voice note turned into spec lines

Voice note: *"Make it feel premium but not stuck up, people should see the classes first thing, and don't make it one of those endless pages."*

Spec lines:
- Look: Editorial direction, warm photography, no gold or black-tie styling.
- Hero shows today's real classes and the trial button above the fold on a phone.
- 7 sections at most; every section scannable in under 10 seconds.
- FAQ answers cancellation and price directly.
